What's happening
Tom has been leading a long-running project that recently lost priority. Adoption challenges and delays have affected motivation and momentum…
Tom joined the team 18 months ago with high enthusiasm, but after a leadership reshuffle the project scope was dramatically cut. He's been showing up but his engagement scores have dropped. He hasn't raised the issue directly — this conversation is your opportunity to open the door.
Talking points
  • Thank Tom for his time
  • Ask Tom about his role and recent activities
  • Ask additional clarifying questions about Tom's motivation behind each decision
  • Clarify reasons behind recent changes in activity
What success looks like
By the end of the conversation:
  • Tom shares specific examples from his experience
  • The conversation feels natural and human, not like an interview
  • Tom leaves feeling heard and supported
